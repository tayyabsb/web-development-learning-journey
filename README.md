🌐 Web Development Learning Journey

«Sometimes, losing your work teaches you something that success never could. 💭»

📖 About This Journey

Last year, I joined an institute called Mustakbil with the goal of specializing in Web Development.

What started as a learning experience turned into almost four months of regular classes, continuous practice, hands-on projects, and exploration of different areas of the web. 💻

During this period, I focused not only on learning how to build websites but also on understanding how the web development ecosystem works in real-world scenarios.

---

💻 What I Explored

Throughout my learning journey, I worked on and explored a wide range of web development areas, including:

- 🌐 Portfolio Websites
- 📝 Blogs
- 📰 News Websites
- 🎓 Learning Management Systems (LMS)
- 🛒 WooCommerce
- 🛍️ E-commerce Websites
- 🏪 Shopify
- 🌐 Online Stores
- 📚 Educational Websites
- 🔎 Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
- 🖥️ WordPress
- 🎨 Wix
- 🏗️ Squarespace

I also built my own portfolio website where I started showcasing the projects I created during my learning process.

---

🧠 Beyond Website Development

My learning experience was not limited to designing and developing websites.

I also gained practical knowledge about:

- Understanding how domains work
- Free and paid domain options
- Purchasing domains
- Choosing and purchasing hosting
- Connecting domains with hosting
- Basic website deployment concepts
- Understanding client requirements
- Communicating with clients
- Working with WordPress
- Exploring different website-building platforms
- Understanding SEO fundamentals

These experiences helped me understand that web development is not only about writing code or designing pages — it also involves problem-solving, communication, deployment, platforms, clients, and continuous learning.

---

💔 The Unexpected Setback

While working on my projects, I used LocalWP to develop and test many of my WordPress websites locally.

Most of my projects were stored on my laptop as local WordPress installations.

Unfortunately, the local project files were deleted from my laptop.

As a result, almost all the projects I had spent months building were lost. 💔

It was difficult because I didn't just lose files.

I lost months of:

- Practical work
- Experiments
- Improvements
- Website projects
- Ideas
- Time and effort

I had planned to showcase those projects on LinkedIn, share my progress, receive feedback, and continue improving them.

Unfortunately, I could no longer show most of that work.

---

📚 What I Didn't Lose

The projects were gone.

But the knowledge wasn't.

Everything I had learned while building those projects — the concepts, experience, problem-solving, mistakes, and practical understanding — remained with me.

That experience taught me an important lesson:

«Your work can be lost, but the skills you gain while creating it stay with you.»

---

🔄 Starting Again

Instead of stopping, I decided to start again.

InshaAllah, I will:

- Rebuild my projects from scratch
- Recreate my portfolio
- Continue improving my Web Development skills
- Strengthen my SEO knowledge
- Build more practical projects
- Document my progress
- Learn from my previous mistakes
- Maintain proper backups of my work

This time, I will also make sure my projects are properly backed up and stored safely so that losing one copy does not mean losing everything.

---

🎯 My Goal

My goal is to continuously improve as a Computer Science student and developer by learning through practical work.

Rather than only learning concepts, I want to:

Learn → Build → Document → Improve → Repeat. 🚀

This repository is part of that journey.

---

❤️ A Lesson I Will Remember

Sometimes you lose the work, but you don't lose the lesson.

The deleted projects reminded me that progress is not only measured by the files you have at the end.

It is also measured by the knowledge, experience, and skills you gain along the way.

Back to building. 💻🚀
